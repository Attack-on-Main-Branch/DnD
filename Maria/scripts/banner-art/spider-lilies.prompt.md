## Prompt

Create a production botanical sprite atlas for an animated fantasy UI banner. Output a high resolution SQUARE 2048x2048 PNG with a truly TRANSPARENT alpha background. Layout: exactly SIX fully isolated individual red spider lily (Lycoris radiata, higanbana) plants, arranged in a strict even 3-column by 2-row invisible grid. Each plant fits completely within its own cell with generous transparent margins, no overlap across cells; all six slender stems end near the bottom of their cell. Each plant has ONE natural rounded umbel of several lily florets at its top, a slender almost black green stem extending well below the flowers, NO leaves or roots. Different natural three-quarter viewing directions and head silhouettes per cell. Make these beautiful sophisticated semi-realistic 3D botanical illustrations: brilliant vermilion scarlet petals, crimson undersides, warm orange-red light on the thin edges, dramatically recurved long narrow ribbon-like petals that curl backward into exquisite loops, and very long fine arching reddish stamens with tiny warm gold pollen tips radiating upward and outward far beyond the petals. Strong botanical silhouette and convincing depth, delicate glossy silk texture, dramatic directional rim lighting, dark burgundy shadows. Think close-up beautiful red spider lilies glowing against darkness: petals are irregular flowing ribbons, NOT flat six-point daisies, NOT scribbled rosettes. The flower head should occupy roughly the upper 45 percent of each tall cell and the leafless stem the remaining lower 40 percent. All filaments and stems must be fully contained, crisp and clearly visible. Pure cutout assets, no ground, no sky, no scene, no backdrop, no moon, no haze, no particles, no detached or falling leaves, no floating petals, no text, no labels, no borders, no watermark. Transparent pixels between and around every plant.
